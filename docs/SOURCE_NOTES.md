# Content basis and editorial decisions

## Inputs

- `New_Portfolio-fixed.zip`: original profile, portrait, project descriptions,
  experience, skills, Fab Lab journeys, contact configuration and resume.
- `Portfolio_Content_Update.zip`: equal-contribution credits, shorter summaries,
  public .NET repository, NimbusMart entry and the two supplied video links.
- `CS50P.pdf`, `AWS.pdf`, `Redhat.pdf`, `Internship Completion.pdf`,
  `AI-ML Training.pdf`: original certificate PDFs. SHA-256 provenance is recorded
  in `source-manifest.json`; the published source PDFs are byte-identical.
- Four supplied certificate photographs: SSGMCE Pursuit workshop, NxtWave,
  IEEE EMBS internship and TCS Tech Day appreciation.

## Decisions grounded in those documents

- Webstack internship: 3 September–20 November 2025, certificate WMSI25_004.
- IEEE EMBS programme: 1–30 June 2026. The site and resume use the dates on the
  supplied certificate, rather than the broader dates in the old portfolio.
- AWS Academy: Cloud Foundations training badge, 20 hours. The source prints
  `05/06/2026`; only the year is displayed so a day/month convention is not guessed.
- Red Hat: RH104 - RHA version 9.1, attendance, 16 credit hours, 2 April 2026.
  This is not labelled as RHCSA/RHCE.
- CS50P: course completion in 2026, nine problem sets and one final project.
- iBase/SSGMCE: 10-day AI/ML industrial training, 26 February–7 March 2026;
  the source is a certificate of participation that states successful completion.
- Pursuit: workshop participation/appreciation, 25–28 March 2026. The decorative
  “Best Award” graphic is not used as evidence of a competition victory.
- TCS: appreciation for Tech Day 2026, not employment or an internship.
- NxtWave: workshop hands-on project completion. The supplied right-edge crop is
  preserved; no missing text or date digit was reconstructed.
- The Fab Lab learning history remains, but no undocumented tenth certificate
  was invented for it.

## Project evidence and limits

The repository URLs, owner-supplied descriptions and earlier reviewed code are the
basis for project case studies. This build did not rerun DermAI training, replicate
ML metrics, or rerun the .NET or NimbusMart application tests. ML numbers are
explicitly labelled project-reported. The known unsafe low-confidence fallback is
retained as a visible limitation, with no clinical-use claim.

The two-sentence equal-contribution credits follow the owner's instruction, naming
Tanushri Khawale and Rahul Paghrut. They do not invent who built individual layers.
An IEEE-format manuscript is not described as an accepted/published IEEE paper.

The project diagrams are labelled pipeline/application overviews. They are not
presented as application screenshots or measured analytics. No product screenshot
or experimental result has been fabricated.

The standalone NimbusMart India patch is separate and was not merged into or
deployed from this portfolio package. Its portfolio entry explicitly notes that
the public demo may still be the earlier version.

Credential links were transcribed from the supplied PDFs. They are not accompanied
by an independently-verified badge. Source PDFs and original photos are accessible
from the relevant document page; only viewing previews and rotated copies were
prepared for presentation.
