Site information

Privacy & contact

A small portfolio, with a small data footprint.

When you send a message

The contact form sends your name, email address, selected topic and message to Web3Forms, which processes the submission for delivery to the portfolio owner. The form does not have a separate application database. Please do not send passwords, payment details, health records or other sensitive information.

You can email anshumanagrawal89@gmail.com directly instead. Contact the same address about messages you have sent or to request deletion from the recipient’s inbox. An API submission success message does not confirm inbox delivery.

Videos and external links

YouTube players load only after you press “Load video.” The player then connects to YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain; the video provider may process connection and viewing data. GitHub, LinkedIn, YouTube and credential-record links take you to their respective services.

Certificate files and preview images are served locally with the portfolio. The website does not add its own analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may keep technical request logs under its own policies.

Documents and project claims

Certificates are labelled as course attendance, completion, internship, workshop or appreciation according to the supplied document. They are not presented as interchangeable professional certifications. Project-reported metrics are identified as such, and research prototypes are not clinical products.

The site presents content reviewed on 1 November 2026. No paid subscription is required to browse it. The source package is available to the owner for maintenance.
