DermAI Monitor

A research prototype for seven-class skin-lesion classification, combining an EfficientNetB3 model, Grad-CAM visualisations and a Streamlit application for reviewing results.

The brief

What this project explores

Explore multiclass skin-lesion classification and make model results reviewable in a supporting application.

Collaboration

My contribution

I co-developed DermAI Monitor with Tanushri Khawale, with both of us contributing equally to the project. Our combined work brought together model development, evaluation and the supporting application.

Implementation

The documented pipeline uses a merged HAM10000 and ISIC 2019 dataset, two-phase transfer learning, focal loss and oversampling. The application adds Grad-CAM visualisation, an ABCD-style score, longitudinal records and PDF reporting.

Evaluation and limitations

The figures above are the project's recorded evaluation results, not evidence of clinical validation. Performance across individual classes and the unsafe low-confidence fallback need to be considered alongside overall accuracy.

Collaboration

The project and accompanying IEEE-format paper were prepared collaboratively. An IEEE-format manuscript is not a claim of IEEE publication or peer-reviewed acceptance.
