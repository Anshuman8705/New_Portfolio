From model to review

Merged HAM10000 and ISIC 2019 data, two-phase transfer learning, focal loss and oversampling support classification. Streamlit adds Grad-CAM, longitudinal records and PDF reports.

Research use only

Not intended for clinical decision-making. The documented low-confidence fallback can label a melanoma image as healthy skin; that limitation must be addressed before considering any real-world use.
