Role & collaboration

Hackathon collaboration with Mohammad Ammar, Kamran Ali and Kartik Hande; later extended with guest study sessions, classrooms and a hosted deployment.

From personal study to a classroom

Teachers share cards through classes with eight-character join codes. Learners copy decks without duplicate imports. Topic selection, bookmarks and confirmed deletion keep study on one page.

Handle a busy AI provider

Bounded retries and a configurable Flash-Lite fallback recover from transient Gemini errors. Validated cards are saved to PostgreSQL; signed sessions keep guest decks separate.

Scope
